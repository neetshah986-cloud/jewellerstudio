Why packaging matters for jewellers

Unboxing is part of the sale

The moment a customer opens the box is part of the experience they are paying for. Good packaging makes it memorable.

The right fit protects the piece

A box made for the shape of your jewellery keeps it safe and presents it neatly.

Gifts are a big part of jewellery

Much jewellery is bought as a gift. Premium packaging makes it ready to give without extra wrapping.

Bags and boxes carry your brand

Every bag that leaves your store is a small advertisement. Good design makes sure it is a good one.

Frequently asked questions

Which types of jewellery boxes do you make?

We make boxes for necklaces, bangles, rings, earrings and pendants, plus gift boxes, rigid boxes, magnetic closure boxes and shipping boxes.

Can you make boxes in custom sizes?

Yes. We design boxes around the size and shape of your products.

Can the packaging carry my logo and special finishes?

Yes. We can add your logo and finishes such as gold or silver foil and embossing. See our commercial printing page for finishes.

Do you design pouches, trays and carry bags too?

Yes. We design pouches, display trays and branded carry bags so your packaging looks consistent.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

It depends on the type of packaging. Tell us the quantity you need and we will advise.

Can you make boxes for corporate gifting?

Yes. We design branded boxes for corporate and client gifts.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp with your product types and the quantity you need.
