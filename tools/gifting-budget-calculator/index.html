How the budget is worked out

  1. Cost per gift = gift + packaging + personalising + delivery.
  2. Gifts to buy = recipients plus a spare percentage, rounded up.
  3. Subtotal = gifts to buy × cost per gift.
  4. GST = rate × subtotal.
  5. Cost per recipient = total ÷ recipients.

Example: 100 recipients, ₹300 gift + ₹60 packaging + ₹40 personalising + ₹25 delivery = ₹425 per gift. With 5% spare you buy 105 gifts: ₹44,625 + 18% GST ₹8,032.50 = ₹52,657.50, which is about ₹526.58 per recipient.

Common questions

How is the gifting budget calculated?

Cost per gift = gift + packaging + personalising + delivery. Extra spare pieces are added to the quantity, then GST is applied on the subtotal. The cost per recipient is the total divided by the number of recipients, so spares are included.

Why add spare pieces?

Some gifts get damaged in packing or transit, and a few extra recipients usually appear at the last minute. Five to ten percent is a common cushion. Set it to zero if you do not need it.

Can I claim GST credit on gifts?

In many cases, GST credit is not available on goods given away as gifts. Rules depend on your situation, so please confirm with your accountant. The calculator shows GST as a cost so your budget is on the safe side.

What does a budget check do?

If you enter a total budget, the tool shows how much is left or by how much you are over, and how many recipients the budget can cover at the current cost per gift.
