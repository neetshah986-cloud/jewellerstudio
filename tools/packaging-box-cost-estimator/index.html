How the estimate works

  1. Quantity = monthly need × months, plus spares, rounded up.
  2. Minimum order raises any small quantity to your supplier’s minimum.
  3. Cost = quantity × rate for each item, added together.
  4. Setup and GST are added to give the total.
  5. Cost per piece sold = total ÷ jewellery pieces sold.

Example: 100 ring boxes a month for 3 months with 5% spare means 315 boxes. At ₹35 each that is ₹11,025 before setup and GST.

Common questions

How is the packaging cost worked out?

For each item, boxes needed = monthly need × months, plus the spare percentage, rounded up. If your supplier has a minimum order, small quantities are raised to it. Cost = quantity × rate. Setup cost and GST are added to get the total.

What is a minimum order quantity?

Many packaging suppliers will only produce a box above a certain quantity because each design needs setup. If you enter a minimum, the calculator raises smaller quantities to that number and tells you.

What affects the price of a jewellery box?

Mainly size, material and finish (for example foil, embossing or magnetic closure), the inserts inside, the number of colours printed, and above all the quantity. Larger runs usually bring the cost per box down.
