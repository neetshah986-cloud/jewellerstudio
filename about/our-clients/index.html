Frequently asked questions

Which kinds of jewellery brands do you work with?

Our clients include gold, silver, diamond, lab-grown and kundan jewellery brands, as you can see from the logos above. Some are single showrooms and some sell in more than one city.

Do you work with jewellers outside Mumbai?

Yes. Our clients are in Mumbai, Ratlam, Indore, Neemuch, Nagpur and Surat in India, and in New York in the USA.

How do I start working with Jeweller Studio?

Message us on WhatsApp with your brand name and what you want to achieve. We reply with a suggested starting point, and you decide what to take forward.
