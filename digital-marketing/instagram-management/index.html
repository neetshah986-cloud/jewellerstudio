Instagram Management for Jewellery Brands

Instagram is where many customers first see your designs. We manage your profile, posts, reels and stories so your page looks polished and gives people an easy way to contact you.

  • Posts, reels and stories handled in one plan
  • Profile that explains who you are within seconds
  • Content built around festivals and the wedding season
Chat on WhatsAppSee what is included

At a glance

  • Best forShowrooms, brands and manufacturers that want a steady, professional Instagram presence
  • You shareProduct photos, new designs and your key dates
  • You getA content plan, designed posts, reels, stories and scheduled posting

What is included in Instagram Management

Profile clean-up

Bio, profile picture, highlights and links arranged so a new visitor understands your brand and how to reach you.

Feed planning

A mix of product posts, carousels and helpful content so your grid looks consistent.

Reels and stories

Short videos and story sets that show detail and keep your page active.

Captions and hashtags

Clear captions and relevant hashtags written for jewellery buyers.

Posting schedule

Everything scheduled on the days and times that suit your audience.

Why it matters for jewellers

Discovery happens visually

People browse designs on Instagram before they ever visit a showroom, so your page often acts as your first shop window.

Consistency builds recognition

A regular, well-designed feed makes your brand easy to remember and easy to trust.

A message is one tap away

A clear profile and good content make it simple for customers to message you or open WhatsApp.

How it works

  1. ReviewWe look at your current profile and what your audience responds to.
  2. PlanWe agree a posting rhythm and content themes.
  3. Create and postWe design, write and schedule your content, with your approval.
  4. ImproveWe review what worked and adjust the next month.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in Instagram management?

Profile set-up, a content plan, designed posts and carousels, reels, stories, captions, hashtags and scheduled posting.

Do you manage Facebook as well?

Yes, we can post across Instagram and Facebook together. See our Facebook Management service.

Do I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. You see the plan and designs first, and we only post what you have approved.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

Our service covers content and posting. Tell us if you want help with replies and we will confirm what we can include.

Do you promise followers or sales?

No. Results depend on your products, pricing and budget. We focus on consistent, good-looking content and open reporting on what works.
