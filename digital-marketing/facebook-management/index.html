Facebook Management for Jewellery Brands

Facebook still reaches many of your local and returning customers. We keep your page active with clear posts, offers and updates, so people know what is new and how to reach you.

  • Regular posts and offers for your local audience
  • Page details kept accurate and complete
  • Festival and wedding season content ready in time

At a glance

  • Best forLocal jewellers and showrooms that want to stay in touch with existing customers
  • You shareStore details, new designs, offers and key dates
  • You getA posting plan, designed posts and scheduled publishing on your Facebook page

What is included in Facebook Management

Page set-up check

Name, address, timings, contact details and cover image checked so customers find correct information.

Content plan

A calendar of new arrivals, offers, festival greetings and useful tips.

Post designs

Branded images and carousels that match your Instagram and print material.

Offer and event posts

Clear announcements for sales, exhibitions and store events.

Scheduled publishing

Posts scheduled in advance so your page never goes quiet.

Why it matters for jewellers

Local customers still use Facebook

Families and returning customers often follow their trusted jeweller on Facebook for updates and offers.

Correct details avoid lost visits

Wrong timings or an old address send customers elsewhere. A maintained page prevents that.

Content can be shared across platforms

The designs we make can also support your Instagram and WhatsApp updates.

How it works

  1. CheckWe review your page details and recent posts.
  2. PlanWe set up a monthly calendar around offers and festivals.
  3. Design and scheduleWe create the posts and schedule them for your approval.
  4. ReviewWe look at what your followers respond to and adjust.

Frequently asked questions

What will you post on my Facebook page?

New arrivals, offers, festival greetings, store updates and helpful jewellery tips, planned monthly.

Can you manage Facebook and Instagram together?

Yes. Many brands combine both so the look and message stay consistent.

Do you run Facebook ads?

Paid ads are a separate service. See Paid Advertising – Meta Ads.

Can you update my business details on the page?

Yes, we check and update details such as address, timings and contact numbers.

How do I get started?
