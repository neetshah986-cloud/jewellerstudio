Why jewellery needs its own social media approach

General social media advice does not always suit jewellery. These are the things we plan around.

Details sell, so visuals must be right

Jewellery is small and full of fine detail. Poor lighting or a cluttered design can make an expensive piece look ordinary, so every post needs careful visuals.

Trust matters as much as style

Customers want to know about purity, hallmarking and certification. Helpful, honest content answers these questions before they walk in or message you.

Buying follows the calendar

Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, Diwali and the wedding season drive much of the year's buying. Your content needs to be ready before each of these moments.

Many sales start with a message

A customer sees a design, then sends a message or opens WhatsApp. Your page should make that next step easy and your replies feel as polished as your jewellery.

Frequently asked questions

What does social media management for jewellers include?

We plan your monthly content, design your posts and carousels, create reels and stories, write captions and hashtags, and schedule everything on your pages. You approve the monthly plan before anything is posted.

Which platforms do you manage?

We manage Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. If you only want to start with one platform, we can begin there and add others later.

How many posts, reels and stories will I get each month?

It depends on your brand, your budget and the season. Most jewellery brands do well with a steady weekly rhythm of posts, reels and stories. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp and we will suggest a plan that fits.

Do you create the product photos and videos too?

We design posts and reels from the photos and videos you share. If you need better visuals, our AI Jewellery Photoshoot and AI Jewellery Reels services can create them, so your feed looks consistent.

Can you plan content for festivals and the wedding season?

Yes. We build the calendar around the dates that matter to jewellers, such as Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, Diwali and the wedding season, and prepare festival creatives before the day arrives.

Do I get to approve the content before it goes live?

Yes. We share the monthly content plan and designs for your approval, and only schedule what you have approved.

How soon will I see results?

Social media works through steady, consistent posting, so expect to build momentum over weeks and months rather than days. We do not promise follower numbers or sales, and we do not buy followers. We focus on content that builds trust and makes it easy for customers to contact you.

Is paid advertising included?

No. Social media management covers organic content. If you want to run paid campaigns, we offer Meta Ads management as a separate service.

Do you work with small jewellers and new brands?

Yes. We work with retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers, bridal and diamond brands, and jewellery start-ups, from single showrooms to growing brands.

How do I get started, and what does it cost?

Message us on WhatsApp with a few details about your brand and what you want to achieve. Pricing depends on the scope of work, so we will share a clear quote after understanding your needs.
